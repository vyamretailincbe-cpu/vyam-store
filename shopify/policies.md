## Return and refund policy

*Effective 26 October 2026*

### Cancellations

You can cancel an order at any time before it is dispatched by writing to vyamretail.incbe@gmail.com with your order number. Cancelled orders are refunded in full, including shipping, to the original payment method.

### Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order number and photos of the product and packaging. We will send a replacement or give a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

### Returns

Our products are household and personal care consumables, so opened or used products cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons, unless they are defective. Unopened products in their original packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery. For non-defective returns, the return shipping cost is paid by the customer and the original ₹100 shipping fee is not refunded.

### Refund timelines

Approved refunds are initiated within 2 business days and credited to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 business days depending on your bank.

### Contact

For any cancellation, return or refund request, write to vyamretail.incbe@gmail.com with your order number.

## Shipping policy

*Effective 26 October 2026*

### Where we ship

We currently ship within India only. We do not accept orders for delivery outside India.

### Shipping charges

A flat shipping fee of ₹100 applies to every order, whatever the number of items. The fee is shown at checkout before you pay.

### Dispatch and delivery times

Orders are dispatched from Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation. Delivery usually takes 3–7 business days depending on your location; remote PIN codes may take longer.

### Tracking

When your order is dispatched, tracking details are sent to the email address or phone number you gave at checkout.

### Delays and issues

If your order hasn't arrived within the expected time, write to vyamretail.incbe@gmail.com with your order number and we'll follow it up with the courier.
